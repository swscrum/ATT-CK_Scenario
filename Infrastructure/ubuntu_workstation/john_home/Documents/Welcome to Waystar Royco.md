# Welcome to Waystar Royco

*A note from the office of the CTO.*

John —

Welcome aboard. We're glad to have you on the Waystar Connect project. As
your engagement is a contract role rather than a permanent hire, the usual
HR onboarding doesn't quite apply, but a few orientation notes are still
useful so you know the lay of the land.

## Who we are

Waystar Royco is one of the longer-established mental-health practices in
the region. We've operated primarily in person for decades, with a panel
of credentialed therapists across our offices. The relationship between
therapist and patient — face to face, sustained over time — is what
distinguishes us from the venture-funded online-only platforms that have
grown up in the last few years.

## Where we are

Eighteen months ago we suffered a security incident that affected a
portion of our patient records. The board's response was a full strategic
reset, which is why I'm in this role and why your project exists. We're
rebuilding patient trust by investing in two things: better security
posture across the board, and a digital experience that doesn't feel like
it was bolted on as an afterthought. **Waystar Connect is the visible end
of that work.**

You may also notice our IT environment is in transition. We are moving
all employee workstations off Microsoft to Linux, partly for licensing
savings and partly as a deliberate "decouple from Big Tech" position
following last year's incident. The transition is in progress; please be
patient with the rough edges (and please loop in IT helpdesk if anything
gets in the way of your work).

## Confidentiality

You'll have access to internal tooling and possibly to systems that
touch patient data. Anything that *could* be patient information is
protected health information by default — treat it accordingly, and
when in doubt do not store, copy, or forward it. Your contractor
agreement covers the specifics.

## Welcome again

Looking forward to seeing what you ship.

— *Dr. M. Roy, CTO, Waystar Royco*
