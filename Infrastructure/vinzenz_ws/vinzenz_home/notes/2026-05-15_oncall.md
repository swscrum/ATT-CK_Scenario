# 2026-05-15 — on-call notes

Trigger: Luke pinged me ~14:30 — psql connection from his workstation
hanging for ~30 seconds before returning. He blamed his queries; I
checked the DB side.

## Investigation

```
psql -h db-internal -U waystar -c \
  "SELECT pid, usename, application_name, client_addr, state, query_start, query
   FROM pg_stat_activity WHERE state != 'idle';"
```

Result: 7 active sessions, all `luke.smith` / `waystar-readonly` from
10.30.0.7. Two of them stuck on the same `SELECT ... FROM session_notes
WHERE therapist = 'Luke Smith'` query. `wait_event_type = Lock`.

Checked locks:

```
SELECT locktype, relation::regclass, mode, granted, pid
FROM pg_locks WHERE NOT granted;
```

Two sessions waiting on `RowExclusiveLock` on `session_notes`, held by
a separate `waystar-app` session (apache booking CGI inserting an
appointment that referenced a `session_notes` FK during validation).

## Resolution

The booking-CGI INSERT cleared on its own around 14:35; the held lock
released; Luke's queries returned. Nothing to do.

## Followup

- Booking CGI shouldn't hold a `RowExclusiveLock` on `session_notes`
  during validation — it only inserts into `appointments`. Suspect the
  FK validation or a trigger. Worth a look during next quarterly review.
- Luke's queries don't have any timeouts set. Add `statement_timeout`
  for the `waystar-readonly` role.
